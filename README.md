# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** created in Microsoft Excel to analyze employee data, workforce distribution, and employee attrition.

The dashboard provides a clear and visual overview of key HR metrics, helping users understand employee trends and identify patterns related to employee turnover.

## 📈 Key Features

- 👥 **Total Employees** – Displays the total number of employees.
- 📉 **Attrition Count** – Shows the total number of employees who have left the organization.
- 📊 **Attrition Rate** – Displays the percentage of employees who have left.
- 💰 **Average Monthly Income** – Shows the average monthly income of employees.
- 🏢 **Employee Count by Department** – Analyzes employee distribution across different departments.
- 👔 **Employee Count by Job Role** – Shows the number of employees in different job roles.
- 💵 **Average Income by Department and Job Role** – Compares employee income across departments and job roles.
- 👥 **Employee Demographics** – Analyzes employee distribution by gender and age group.
- 💍 **Attrition by Marital Status** – Examines employee attrition based on marital status.
- ⏰ **Attrition by Overtime** – Compares attrition between employees who work overtime and those who do not.
- ⭐ **Job Satisfaction Analysis** – Displays job satisfaction ratings across different job roles.
- 🔎 **Interactive Filters** – Filter dashboard data by:
  - Department
  - Gender
  - Job Role
  - Age Group

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers / Interactive Filters
- Excel Formulas
- Data Visualization

## 🎯 Purpose of the Dashboard

The main purpose of this dashboard is to transform raw HR data into an **easy-to-understand visual report**. It helps users monitor workforce distribution, analyze employee attrition, compare income across departments and job roles, and explore employee satisfaction and overtime trends.

## 🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify:

- Overall employee count and attrition rate
- Departments with the highest number of employees
- Average monthly income across different departments and job roles
- Employee attrition trends by gender and age group
- Attrition patterns based on marital status and department
- Differences in attrition between employees who work overtime and those who do not
- Job satisfaction ratings across different job roles
- Employee distribution across salary slabs

This Excel HR Analytics Dashboard demonstrates how **Pivot Tables, interactive filters, charts, and KPIs** can be combined to create an effective HR reporting solution.
